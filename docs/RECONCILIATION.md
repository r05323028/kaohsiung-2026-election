# Reconciliation

## Purpose

`reports/election-reconciliation.json` and `.md` are deterministic evidence of what passed the publication gate at the checked-in snapshot. They are generated by `scripts/validate-official-data.ts`, not hand-edited.

## Current comparison

The validator compares:

1. CEC article 65213 qualified Kaohsiung mayor names.
2. KHEC article 65366 drawing metadata and retained PDF identity.
3. CEC article 63625 constituency/seat definition represented in the approved district list.
4. The approved canonical dataset and its source/checksum manifest.

The current report is `partial`: five mayor names match; all 16 official districts are represented; 101 council rows match the normalized source across 15 constituencies; and one council-announcement scope remains pending. The council rows are published only as preliminary drawn/non-final entries, with party information not published. They are not the 2026-11-17 formal announcement.

## Approval gate

Promotion to `data/approved/` requires:

- schema validation and unique stable IDs;
- all source and district references resolve;
- approved candidates have approved qualification;
- non-final numbers cannot carry `candidateNumberIsFinal: true`;
- independently retained roster matches normalized approved identities;
- pending scopes are explicit and do not leak into candidate routes/search;
- checksum mismatch fails the offline validator.

Discrepancies produce `status: partial` or a thrown validation error depending on whether the record is safely withheld. A safe partial report is not permission to publish withheld records.

After the scheduled 2026-11-12 mayor and 2026-11-17 council announcements, retrieve new primary snapshots, review changes, reconcile again, and only promote records whose final status is supported. Do not mutate the old snapshot in place without recording the new retrieval/checksum.
